### 🎯 Purpose & Scope
Verify the absolute mathematical, computational, and regulatory fidelity of the application's domain logic. Ensures that business rules and calculation algorithms strictly comply with formal specifications, domain invariants, and eliminate arbitrary heuristics or unverified assumptions.
